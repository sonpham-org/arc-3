RL v2 · one mode per turn

A coach that picks how the next turn is played

At every new turn the coach picks a mode: a one-line focus added to the turn message, plus a thinking budget, a move cap and a temperature. Builds are the harness and coach versions; decisions are the modes it picked, turn by turn; sampling is how often each mode is picked in each situation, and how that went.

01

Builds

Each card is one build: a harness, or a coach on top of its parent. The big number is the mean all-25 score over finished runs; under a child, the change against its parent. The small chips are single runs; a run still playing shows its minute. Click a run with decisions to inspect it.

02

Decisions

One run, one game: every mode the coach picked, in order. The strip shows the whole game at a glance (a tick marks a turn that cleared a level, a dark base a game over). Click a row for everything the coach saw.

03

Sampling

Rows are situations (where in the game the turn came), columns are modes. "Share" is how often the build picked each mode there. "Level within 30" is the share of those turns followed by a level within 30 actions, minus the same for stock turns: blue better, red worse, grey when fewer than 20 turns back it.

Game ids are opaque on purpose.
