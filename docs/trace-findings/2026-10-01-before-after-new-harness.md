All runs here are Flash-Next (pruned), all 25 public games, one pass, the same 132-minute clock. "The
hard seven" in this report always means the **Flash-Next bottom seven**: Skewer Kebabs, Buoyant
Pontoons, Locksmith, Ghost Twin, Leapfrog, Warehouse Associates, Toggle Navigator.

- **Overall the new harness is ahead, and the gap is bigger than the swing.** Against the halfway runs
  it is about 8 levels and 4.5 points of mean score. Run-to-run spread inside the new group is about
  4 levels and 3 points; inside the halfway group about 2 and 2. The two ranges barely touch: the best
  halfway run (104 levels) edges the two worst new runs (103 each) and ties a third; the other nine
  new runs are above every halfway run. On mean score the best halfway run (42.2) beats three of the
  twelve new runs.
- **On the hard seven the new harness as a whole did not move.** 14.5 against 13.5 levels with a
  spread of 2 to 3 on each side is no difference. The one exception is the three runs that carry the
  Daniel bundle v2 (section 4): they cleared 17, 18 and 19 hard-seven levels; the other nine new runs
  cleared 9 to 17 (typical 14), the same as halfway.
- **The big jump happened before the new harness, not with it.** Old to halfway is about 22 levels and
  14 points, and those halfway runs differ from the old ones in the model repair net, the draft head and
  the gameplay temperature as well as in harness pieces. This report cannot split that jump between
  model and harness.
- **How big is "run-to-run swing" really?** The only true repeats of one setup in this data are two
  old-harness pairs, and they swung a lot: 92 against 78 levels (16 lanes) and 88 against 73 (19 lanes).
  The later groups are much tighter, but they are made of different arms, not repeats. Son's notebook
  notes give four-run averages on his own cloud arm, which are lower than the single runs on the site:
  the giant candidate averages 107 levels and 44.4 where the one published giant run shows 113 and 48.3.
  Read every single-run number below as "give or take four or five levels".
- **Mean score and levels agree here.** The 4.5-point gap over halfway comes mostly from Compass Dye,
  Trail Unwind, Mirror Rendezvous, Sequence Belt and Functional Tiles scoring higher, with one large
  loss the other way on Reaching Lurch (section 3).

### Confounds, stated once

- **Model cut and temperature move with the harness.** Every new-harness run is the 336 cut (or the
  per-layer 336 cut) at temperature 0.6. Five of the six halfway runs and all old runs are the 384 cut,
  at 0.7 or 0.8 where named. The one halfway run on the same cut and temperature as the champion
  (`p336-t06-fixes-td`, 101 levels / 39.1) is the cleanest single before-and-after: the champion adds
  the honest restart at 60 s and scores 109 / 44.2. One run each.
- **Lanes and context differ** (16 or 19 lanes, 79k to 103k context) across all three groups.
- **The two queue runs end differently.** In `q19-a` and `giant-c` every unfinished game is marked
  cancelled by the shared clock (17 to 18 games), where the other runs mostly show the game giving up on
  its own clock. Their level counts are still comparable; their per-game clock is not.
- **Single runs.** No arm in the new group was repeated on the site. Everything in section 4 is a
  one-run reading.
- **Held-out games** (Axis Reflectors, Reach Emblems, Sequence Belt, Sucking Up, Toggle Runes, Trail
  Unwind, Volume Control): scores are reported; their transcripts were not read for this report and
  must never be used for training.

## 3. Where a new run and an earlier run parted ways

The literal first turn where two runs differ is turn one to three in every pair - the opening probes
are random at this temperature - so it says nothing. What is shown instead is **the level where the
earlier run stalled and the new run got through**: what the new run was thinking on the turn that
cleared it, and what the earlier run believed when it stopped. One pair per game, chosen as the best
new run against a typical earlier run (the matched 336 / 0.6 halfway run where it fits). These pairs
illustrate; they are not a sample. Quotes are the model's own words, trimmed.

**Skewer Kebabs (hard seven)** - `giant-c` (3 levels) against halfway `p384-t07-fixes-td` (0). Parted
