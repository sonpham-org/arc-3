Alternative interactive reasoning environments

Beyond the Public 25

What does ARC-AGI-3's public demonstration set make visible—and which complementary reasoning demands can new games isolate without becoming obscure, linguistic, or brute-forceable?

Working research program · 29 August 2026

Epistemic boundary. The public 25 are intentionally not representative of the private benchmark. “Missing” below always means sparse or absent in the publicly observable evidence, never proven absent from private ARC-AGI-3.

ARC-AGI-3 asks an agent to discover an unstated objective, learn unfamiliar action semantics, build a causal model from interaction, and execute a plan efficiently. That is a much richer object of study than a final score.

The official technical report describes a public demonstration set of 25 environments and two private sets of 55. The public set is the front door: intentionally clearer, more playful, and less mechanically comprehensive than the private evaluation. Our question is therefore not “what did ARC Prize forget?” It is “what additional controlled environments would let us study under-observed forms of exploration, revision, memory, planning, and transfer?”

Five corpora, five different jobs

Counts do not imply benchmark quality. Provenance determines how each corpus may be used.

Coverage is a hypothesis, not a tag cloud

A useful mechanic label says what information must be acquired and what kind of model turns that information into action. “Maze,” “puzzle,” and “click” describe surfaces. “Active causal intervention,” “persistent identity,” and “distributed partial observability” describe reasoning demands.

The separate 63-idea ledger is already unusually strong because it records a primary mechanic axis and an anticipated AI failure mode. It also reveals its own sampling bias: portal topology, cyclic time, irreversibility, and action order dominate; several embodied and quantitative axes have a single seed.

Audit rule. A matrix cell is filled only after source inspection, controlled play, or a replay establishes the mechanic. Visual resemblance is not evidence.

The GPT partition contains ten candidate concepts for each of these twenty families. Balance at ideation is only a guardrail. Concepts can still be rejected, merged, or reassigned after novelty review; no quota can turn a weak reskin into a valid environment.

Current inventory. GPT concept ledger: 200. External seed ledger: 63. Anthropic implementation queue: 200 GPT-seeded briefs across 25 additional families. Only qualified environments count toward the final 200/200.
02

Prototype one: observation as an actuator

q001 does not ask where an object is hidden. It asks what being seen causes the object to do.

Magenta crescents are shy: a pulse advances them only when a wall or shutter blocks the observer. Yellow bursts are bold: a pulse advances them only while visible. The goal is to stop every piece on its hollow socket at the same time.

This makes observation causal. Moving the observer does not advance the world; it changes which transition function the next pulse will apply.

  • Eight deterministic levels.
  • Known-win and known-loss recordings.
  • Exact replay frame hashes.
  • 5,000-action seeded crash/frame fuzzer.
  • Packaged as a standard offline ARC environment.

One game at a time, several games in flight

Every environment moves through specification, deterministic qualification, internal play, external human calibration, sealing, and only then held-out evaluation. A developer can keep three or four environments at different stages, but implementation remains serial at the quality gate: the next game does not inherit “done” status from a generator.

Each level has a named instructional role: orient, discriminate, plan, remember, compose, inhibit, transfer, or synthesize. Increasing board size or object count without a new reasoning demand is rejected.

Acceptance floor. At least six levels; deterministic solution replay; no text or cultural symbols; non-tutorial random-policy success below 1 in 10,000; human-solvable within a bounded session; visual and solver-structure novelty.

Testing the value of a harness

Default ARC3 and Tufa runs must be paired on the same model, game version, seed, inference configuration, and time/action budget. Otherwise a harness comparison is confounded with model or environment variance.

The primary estimate is the paired difference in human-normalized environment score. Levels completed, actions, tokens per effective action, repeated-state rate, no-impact actions, and hypothesis revisions explain where that difference came from. Confidence intervals bootstrap games, not individual actions.

Do not rank from one pass. Existing ARC3 runs show large game-level stochastic variance. The study uses at least three diagnostic seeds and reports paired uncertainty.
