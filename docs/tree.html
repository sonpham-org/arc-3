Tree explorer

Every play of a game as a tree. Plays share only the game start, so that is the root and each play is its own branch. Length is moves: a dot marks where a level was cleared, a cross where the play got stuck, so a better branch is a shorter one. Forks of one play will hang off its branch. Click a stretch to read its turns, a dot for the board there.
