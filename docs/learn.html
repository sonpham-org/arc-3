ARC-3 · A guide for players and helpers

Learn one thing at a time.

You do not need to know the answer before you start.

Try one move. Look for one change. Use what you learn.

How to use this guide together

Pick one game. Read one step aloud, if that helps.

Let the player point to their answer. They can also say it.

Ask one question. Give them time to look and try.

If they get stuck, point to one useful part. Then try again.

Keep a short note: “When I did ___, ___ changed.”

A new board may bring a new rule. Stop and look again.

These are practice ideas made from Astra's published notes. Some notes leave out the answer. A helper note says where that happens.

Being able to notice a change is useful, even before a win.
