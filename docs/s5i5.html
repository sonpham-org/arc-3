One game, followed through the training rounds

On s5i5 the model learned to go further

s5i5 is a puzzle with 8 levels: you click colour-matched buttons to grow, shrink and later rotate rods, carrying markers onto pins before a click budget runs out. Each round the model played it six times, then trained on its own plays. Before training it never cleared more than 3 of the 8 levels. After round 1 it cleared 6 or 7 in four of six attempts, including levels its training had never shown solved.

Rounds 1 and 2 used the first training recipe. It was later found to make the model copy-paste its own reasoning on other games, so those rounds are archived on the RL page, but they are kept here: s5i5 is the clearest sign so far that training teaches the model something.
