# ah25 — copy of ar25 Axis Reflectors (HELD-OUT, TEST ONLY — NEVER TRAIN)

**Kept.** ar25's own class drives every step (renamed and subclassed): arrows move the selected
piece or mirror one cell (a vertical mirror only sideways, a horizontal one only up and down),
Cycle and click select, Undo, every piece is reflected live across every mirror, the level ends
when every target cell is covered, and each level has the original's step budget. Eight levels
with the same pieces, the same mirrors and mirror kinds (fixed vertical on 1, movable vertical on
2, horizontal on 3–4, both from 5), so each mechanic arrives on the same level.

**Changed.** Every board is mirrored or half-turned — only the transforms that keep vertical
mirrors vertical. Every level has a **new target shape**: the movable pieces and mirrors are given
a new arrangement whose total distance from the start is at least the distance the original's own
solution moves them; the target is what that arrangement covers (reflections only on levels 1–2,
as in the original; pieces and reflections from level 3, as in the original), minus anything
already covered at the start; every piece and movable mirror must be needed, and the target keeps
at least 60% of the original's cell count. The winning line (click to select, arrows to move) is
proven in the engine. Levels 1–2 are certified by exact breadth-first search: fewest steps 15
against 15, and 12 against 11. The step bar moves from the right column to the left column. All
colours are reassigned (white field, dark-red targets, orange mirrors).

**Not copied faithfully / caveats.** Tile scale is unchanged: the rules' own overlay draws
reflections only at the 21-cell board's scale. Piece art is unchanged: the rules read piece
pixels for coverage. Levels 3–8 are not certified by search (two pieces and two mirrors are too
many states); their difficulty is bounded by the intended arrangement's distance from the start,
not measured exactly, so some may admit a shorter solution than the original.
