# th87 — copy of tr87 Toggle Runes (HELD-OUT, TEST ONLY — NEVER TRAIN)

**Kept.** The original rule code, copied programmatically: left/right move the bracket, up/down
cycle the selected glyph (or, on the editable-rule levels, every glyph of the selected rule
half) through its family of seven; a source row must translate, rule by rule and left to right,
into the target row; the same move budget per level; six levels with the same flags on the same
levels — plain translation on 1–3, two-step translation on 4, editable rules on 5, editable rules
with tree and two-step translation on 6. Each level keeps the original's rule shapes (how many
glyphs on each side of every rule) and row lengths.

**Changed.** All 21 glyph shapes are new and smaller (4×4 on a 6-pixel pitch, original 5×5 on
7); family tiles 6×6 (original 7×7); colours moved by one permutation (white field, light-grey
panel, dark-red glyphs, green/purple/red family tiles, black bracket); rule cards are re-packed
into different rows and columns and the panel divider moves; the budget bar is the left column
(original: bottom row); new seeds for glyph rotations and the starting scramble. Content: levels
1–4 have new rule sets and new source rows; level 5 has a new intended rule assignment with a new
source and target.

**Not copied faithfully / caveat.** Level 6 keeps the original's puzzle up to relabelling: each
glyph family is cyclically renumbered and drawn with new shapes, the layout and scramble are new,
but the underlying solution structure is the original's. Building a fresh level 6 needs a solver
for the combined tree + two-step + editable-rule check, which was not written; a cyclic relabel is
the only relabelling that keeps the level solvable, because editing a rule half shifts all its
glyphs together.

**Difficulty.** Stored winning lines (actions): 13, 19, 18, 20, 19, 37 against 17, 26, 26, 21,
14, 24 in a recorded win of the original. These are not shortest solutions on either side.
