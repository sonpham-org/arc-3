# vh33 — copy of vc33 Volume Control (HELD-OUT, TEST ONLY — NEVER TRAIN)

**Kept.** vc33's own class drives every step (renamed and subclassed): clicking a pump moves one
step of liquid through a wall, liquid settles toward the level's gravity side, riders sit on the
liquid surface and must line up with the mark of their colour on a wall beside their tank,
ferries carry riders across, and each level has the original's click budget. Seven levels with
the same tanks, pumps, riders, ferries and budgets, so every mechanic arrives on the same level.

**Changed.** Every level is turned (a quarter, half or three-quarter turn, chosen per level)
together with its gravity — the rules are written for any gravity direction, so a turned level
is the same machine. Levels 1–3 and 6 have **new goals**: the marks move to rider positions that
breadth-first search through the real engine first reaches no sooner than the original's fewest
clicks, and the copy's fewest clicks are then measured the same way and must not be lower
(copy/original: 4/3, 9/7, 24/23, 21/20). Levels 1–2 are drawn at half the tile size (the view
widens from 32 to 40); other views are re-padded. The budget bar moves from the top row to the
bottom row. All colours are reassigned (dark-red background, light-grey liquid, pink frame).

**Not copied faithfully / caveats.** Levels 4, 5 and 7 keep the original goal, turned: on level 4
an exhaustive search found no other rider position as far from the start as the original goal,
and levels 5 and 7 have state spaces too large to certify a new goal's difficulty in reasonable
time. Level 6 is shifted, not turned, because its rules special-case leftward gravity of strength
3. Pump, rider and ferry art is unchanged: the rules read rider pixels (the colour key) and click
hits on those sprites.
