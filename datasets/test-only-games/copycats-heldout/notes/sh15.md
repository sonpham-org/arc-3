# sh15 — copy of su15 Sucking Up (HELD-OUT, TEST ONLY — NEVER TRAIN)

**Kept.** su15's own class drives every step (renamed and subclassed): a click pulls every piece
within reach onto the click and costs a step; same-tier clusters merge one tier up the size
chain; mixing tiers is a foul with escalating penalties; creatures chase the nearest block and
shrink what they touch; deliveries into the circles must match the shopping list exactly; the
header rows refuse clicks. Nine levels with the same pieces, creatures, circles, budgets and
shopping lists per level, so each mechanic arrives on the same level.

**Changed.** Every level is mirrored left to right (the legends move to the right-hand side of
the header). Every playfield is reworked: blocks, creatures and circles are nudged to new spots
and up to three small decoy blocks are parked away from every click, and an edit is kept only if
the original's recorded winning clicks, mirrored, still clear the level exactly on the last
click. The step bar moves from the bottom row to the right column and fills upward. All colours
are reassigned (light-grey field, dark-red header, new tier colours).

**Not copied faithfully / caveats.** Tile scale is unchanged: the rules hard-code the 64×64
field (click maths, header rows, pull reach). Block shapes are unchanged: tiers are told apart by
size and colour and catching tests piece pixels. On level 7 the mirrored clicks sit one pixel
further left than a pure mirror, because the pull rounds piece centres. The winning line has the
original's length per level (11, 13, 15, 9, 28, 9, 6, 11, 12 clicks); the field around it is new,
but because the nudges stay within the pull's reach the layout is a close variant rather than a
new puzzle.
