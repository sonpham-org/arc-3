# rh86 — copy of re86 Reach Emblems (HELD-OUT, TEST ONLY — NEVER TRAIN)

**Kept.** re86's own class drives every step (renamed and subclassed): arrows slide the selected
piece three pixels, ACTION5 hands control to the next piece, pieces pass through each other,
colour pads repaint a piece, walls bend crosses and squash hollow squares, a level clears when
every dot's centre is covered by a piece of its colour, and each level has the original's step
budget. Eight levels with the same pieces, pads, walls and budgets, so every mechanic arrives on
the same level (new piece shapes on 2–3, pads on 4, walls on 6).

**Changed.** Every level is turned or mirrored — the first square symmetry, from a per-level
order, under which the original's recorded winning line, transformed, still clears. Every level
has **new dots**: each piece is driven (over a pad of a new colour wherever the original forces
repainting) to a new resting place whose total distance from the start is at least what the
original's own solution moves the pieces; the dots are stamped onto the pieces as they finally
lie (bent or squashed shapes included), in the original's number, never on a pixel the start
position already satisfies; the driving line is replayed through the engine and trimmed at the
clear. The step bar moves from the bottom row to the top row. All colours are reassigned (light-
blue field).

**Not copied faithfully / caveats.** Tile scale is unchanged: the rules move pieces three pixels
on a fixed 64-pixel board. Piece, pad and wall art is unchanged: the rules read their pixels.
Difficulty is bounded by the intended displacement, not certified by search (the state spaces
are large); dots can cluster on fewer pieces than in the original when a piece cannot reach a
new pad.
