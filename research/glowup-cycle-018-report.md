## Baseline audit

q031-v1 had six levels, no real action animation, no recoverable error, tiny
color-led machine selection, and several declared late operators that its
known solutions never used. L3 replaced rather than composed the earlier
split, while L5 and L6 did not need their claimed rotate or balance demands.
The conserved-total invariant was sound, but the game felt like an unfinished
mechanic sketch rather than a progressive instrument.

q129-v2 already had eight levels and a strong prepare-then-retrieve loop, but
remote echo pollen and global fade settled without their own causal cues. Its
gameplay-critical free night allowance was hidden, guard ties used an
unexplained stable ordering, invalid moves could trigger unlimited free recoil,
and two nearly identical garden maps made traversal prefixes feel repeated.

Six independent simulated perspectives produced baseline medians of 40.5/100
for q031-v1, 90/100 for q129-v2, and 95/100 pair distance. These perspectives
are adversarial design aids, not human playtesting.

## Game changes

`q031-v2`, Cryogenic Manifold, makes conserved quantity manipulation a tactile
two-selector laboratory. One selector chooses an explicitly directed conduit;
the other chooses a shape-coded machine. Unit drip, capacity-limited flush,
empty-destination split, rail coupler, alternating phase interlock, mirrored
manifold transfer, check-valve direction, exact balance, and whole-rack rotor
compose into inspectable target states. Quantities are redundant meniscus and
lozenge stacks; target topology and phase are visible in a separate violet
blueprint. Blocked experiments cost no budget, and two audit crystals make one
wrong final check recoverable.

All eight levels introduce or compose real demands. Independent forbidden-
machine BFS proves no within-budget solution without L2 flush, L3 slide, L4
split, L5 phase, or L6 manifold. L7 separately requires split, slide, rotor,
and flush; L8 requires manifold, phase, and rotor. The low-key instrument uses
chamfered glass, triangular valves, diamonds, coils, trusses, capacity datums,
technical tethers, rectilinear packets, rail slides, diagnostic sweeps, and
rotary orbits instead of raw tanks and instant state cuts.

`q129-v3`, Velvet Moth Masque, retains the daylight belief-authorship and
night retrieval identity while making its causal model explicit. Muted owls
preview exactly which shapes would be protected, including deterministic tie
order. Remote silk echo travels to the affected flower; old wreaths visibly
fold inward under decay. One-use dew erases the latest scent and a new one-use
triangular prism copies it to the opposite species. Paid refusals are bounded
and terminal on the third misuse, while an exact gold fringe exposes remaining
free night flight.

Six distinct level grids across five organic stage families replace the nearly
fixed map: open court, split twine beds, pinched grove, revolving side aisles,
dew beds, and a prismatic lower stage. The game uses round, diamond, triangle,
and star flowers, leaf-cluster hedges, scalloped curtains, pollen blooms, dew
ripples, split prism rays, bed turns, winged glides, and corner blossoms.
Actual traces exercise echo on L2-L8, fade on L3-L8, two watchers on L4-L8,
rotation on L5-L8, dew on L6-L8, and prism on L7-L8.

The Flash review supplied mechanic atoms only. q031 transforms `f028` exact
flow, `fl007` topology-controlled mixing, and `fl014` sliding infrastructure
into a discrete cryogenic operator curriculum. q129 transforms `f018` tactile
teaching, `f040` prepare-then-endure, `fl055` action-conditioned audit, and
`fl061` demonstrated adversary policy into a noncombat botanical masque. No
source names, art, characters, layouts, code, stories, or level sequences were
copied.

warm, saturated, organic paper theatre, and local reversible machine commits

## Qualification evidence

shortest plan with BFS, finds a one-error recovery on all sixteen levels,
verifies finite losses (`6,6` for q031 and `5,5,5` for q129), and computes the

proof visits 2,845,233 cached states.

and budget parity. Every consequential action returns six to nine frames,
contains multiple unique frames, and ends on the exact stable engine render.

checks q129's four non-color flower silhouettes and six distinct topologies,
