# Glow-up cycle 019: Festival Scaffold and Moonpool Balance Web

Cycle 019 sampled `q051-v1` and `q038-v2` uniformly outside the rolling
last-ten window. The baseline pair was structurally high-risk because both
used sparse node-edge resource networks, but the final gameplay-distance gate
passed without a redraw: direct pointer-authored construction followed by a
batch stress audit is now genuinely distinct from sequential conserved-fluid
routing through immutable infrastructure. This cycle activates `q051-v2` and

## Baseline audit

`q051-v1` had six levels, mostly one-frame state cuts, raw bars and square
pins, budget-costing unused controls and missed clicks, immediate loss on a
first bad audit, and later stages that mostly enlarged the same max-flow task.
Its finite-stock load-bearing core was sound, but fidelity scored a simulated
median 41/100.

`q038-v2` already had eight levels, exact staged routing, 257 campaign frames,
and a strong dark organic identity. Its ordinary selector/reverse animations
were only four frames, the selected strand alternated full and dotted geometry
in a flashing pattern, and mistakes lacked the deliberate persistent recovery
language required by the current rubric. Baseline fidelity was 87.5/100.

The baseline pair scored 61/100 distance. Retaining it was conditional on
preserving q051 as direct spatial infrastructure authorship and q038 as fixed-
web execution; the final games satisfy that condition without concept damage.

## Game changes

`q051-v2`, Festival Scaffold, makes every candidate span directly coordinate-
addressable and every installed member reclaimable. Timber, sagging cable, and
riveted steel have shape-coded structural roles. Exact capacity, independent
paths, complete gusset triangles, tension/compression compatibility, marked
timber buckling, and multiple moving-load or wind stress cases become necessary
across eight levels. L7 and L8 synthesize at least three earlier demands.

Its high-key warm elevation uses masonry feet, arched crowns, round bolts,
grain-striped timber, bead-ended cable, steel chords, diamond gussets, hanging
pennant loads, scalloped canopy cloth, and shaped stock samples. Members grow
bolt-first or retract; a longer autonomous audit sends a stress wave through
the completed pavilion before success or slump. Blocked pointer clicks cost no
budget, two bad audits produce visible seals before finite loss, and every
valid construction error can be reversed.

`q038-v3`, Moonpool Balance Web, preserves the selector/reverse/pump identity
and never permits construction or coordinate placement. Ordered distribution
milestones compose unequal basin capacities, reversible currents, one-way
valves, alternating tide gills, linked secondary pulses, and a late indivisible
two-droplet braid. The final seven-stage route requires the braid, gate, linked
handoff, direction scheduling, capacity headroom, and final audit.

Its low-key saturated aquarium uses offset circular pools, petal capacities,
inner droplets, outer target halos, curved woven filaments, valve fins, gills,
interlocked knots, double braids, plankton, shells, and tide shapes. A local
scout bead now advances monotonically along selection paths; it never flashes
the whole edge. Pumps, counter-swims, cascade handoffs, gate rings, shell
closures, pool blooms, and drains all settle visibly. Three persistent shells
make early mistakes recoverable before a finite terminal loss.

The Flash review supplied mechanic atoms only. q051 transforms `f021`, `f023`,
`f026`, `f031`, and `f040` into an exact coordinate-snapped structural proof.
q038 transforms `f028`, `f038`, `f041`, and `fl004` into a staged conserved-
droplet scheduler. No source names, art, characters, layouts, code, stories,
or level sequences were copied.
